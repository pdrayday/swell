Peanut Butter Cup Protein Ice Cream

Peanut butter and chocolate ice cream from a single Swell Peanut Butter Cup shake.

Protein
30g
Prep
5 min
Freeze
24 hr
Makes
1 pint

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle (14 fl oz) Swell Peanut Butter Cup protein shake
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter, warmed
  • 1 tbsp chopped dark chocolate

Steps

  1. Pour. Shake the bottle, then pour the whole Swell Peanut Butter Cup into an empty Creami pint.
  2. Freeze. Lid it and freeze flat for 24 hours.
  3. Spin. Spin on Lite Ice Cream.
  4. Re-spin if crumbly. Add 1 tbsp milk and run Re-spin once.
  5. Mix in. Make a well in the middle, add mix-ins and run Mix-in.

Tip: Drizzle warm peanut butter into the hole after the first spin, then run Mix-in.
