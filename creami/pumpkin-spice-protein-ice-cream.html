Pumpkin Spice Protein Ice Cream

Pumpkin pie in a pint, made from Swell Pumpkin Spice.

Protein
30g
Prep
5 min
Freeze
24 hr
Makes
1 pint

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle (14 fl oz) Swell Pumpkin Spice protein shake
  • 1 graham cracker, crumbled

Steps

  1. Pour. Shake the bottle, then pour the whole Swell Pumpkin Spice into an empty Creami pint.
  2. Freeze. Lid it and freeze flat for 24 hours.
  3. Spin. Spin on Lite Ice Cream.
  4. Re-spin if crumbly. Add 1 tbsp milk and run Re-spin once.
  5. Mix in. Make a well in the middle, add mix-ins and run Mix-in.

Tip: A pinch of cinnamon on top after spinning makes it taste like the pie crust.
