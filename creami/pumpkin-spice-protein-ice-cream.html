Pumpkin Spice Protein Ice Cream

Pumpkin pie in a pint, made from Swell Pumpkin Spice.

Protein
30g
Prep
5 min
Freeze
24 hr
Makes
1 pint

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle (14 fl oz) Swell Pumpkin Spice protein shake
  • 1 graham cracker, crumbled

Steps

  1. Pour. Shake the bottle, then pour the whole Swell Pumpkin Spice into an empty Ninja Creami pint.
  2. Freeze. Lid it and freeze on a flat surface for 24 hours.
  3. Spin. Lock the pint in and spin on Lite Ice Cream.
  4. Re-spin if crumbly. If it looks powdery, add 1 tbsp milk and run Re-spin once.
  5. Mix in. Make a well in the center, add mix-ins and run Mix-in.

Tip: A pinch of cinnamon on top after spinning makes it taste like the pie crust.
