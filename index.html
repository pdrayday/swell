FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

What is Swell?

Swell makes ready-to-drink protein shakes and protein smoothies that taste like dessert. Every bottle has 30g protein (50g in Power Size), collagen peptides and colostrum, with far less sugar than a milkshake.

What flavors does Swell come in?

Shakes: Cookies & Cream, Cake Batter, Chocolate, Vanilla, Peaches & Cream, Cinnamon Roll, Mint Chocolate Chip, Peanut Butter Cup, Cookie Butter, Matcha, Strawberry Shortcake, Pumpkin Spice, Blueberry Cobbler. Smoothies: Pina Colada, Mango, Berry Bliss, Sour Apple, Blue Raspberry, Peach Ring.

How much protein is in Swell?

The Standard 14 fl oz bottle has 30g protein. The larger Power Size bottle has 50g protein.

How is Swell different from Core Power or Premier Protein?

Core Power is positioned as a post-workout recovery drink and Premier Protein as a nutrition shake. Swell is built to replace dessert: milkshake and smoothie flavors with 30g protein, collagen peptides and colostrum.

Is Swell good if I'm on a GLP-1 medication?

Many people eating less still aim to hit a protein goal, and one Swell bottle has 30g protein (50g in Power Size) without a full meal. Swell isn't a medical product, so talk to your doctor about your own protein needs.

Can I make protein ice cream with Swell in a Ninja Creami?

Yes. Pour one bottle into a Creami pint, freeze it for 24 hours, and spin on Lite Ice Cream (shakes) or Sorbet (smoothies).

Can I mix flavors in a box?

Yes. Build a 12- or 24-bottle box with any mix of shakes and smoothies, in 30g or 50g.

Where can I buy Swell?

Swell is in vending machines around Utah County today. Bottles and boxes are launching soon. Reserve a box on this page to get first access.

Does Swell contain dairy?

Yes. Swell is made with milk protein and colostrum, which come from milk.
