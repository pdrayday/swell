What's inside

Three things, no filler.

Here's what goes into every bottle and why it's there. Tap any ingredient to read more.

25g protein

Milk protein

Each 14 fl oz bottle has 25 grams of protein. That's about what you'd get from four eggs, in a shake that tastes like dessert.

Milk protein makes up the base, which is what gives Swell its thick, creamy milkshake texture.

Collagen peptides

Peptides

Collagen peptides are collagen protein broken into small pieces so they dissolve smoothly with no taste or grit.

Collagen is the most abundant protein in the body, found in skin, tendons and joints. Swell adds it on top of the milk-protein base.*

Colostrum

From milk

Colostrum is the first milk produced after a calf is born. It's naturally rich in proteins, immunoglobulins and growth factors.

Because it comes from milk, it fits naturally into a milk-based shake.*

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

What is Swell?

Swell is a ready-to-drink protein shake that tastes like a milkshake. Each 14 fl oz bottle has 25g protein and far less sugar than a typical milkshake, plus collagen peptides and colostrum.

What flavors does Swell come in?

Swell launches in four flavors: Cookies & Cream, Cake Batter, Chocolate and Vanilla.

How much protein is in a Swell shake?

Every 14 fl oz bottle of Swell has 25 grams of protein.

What are collagen peptides and colostrum?

Collagen peptides are collagen protein broken into small, easy-to-dissolve pieces. Colostrum is the first milk produced after calving and is naturally rich in proteins and antibodies. Swell adds both to its milk-protein base.

How does Swell compare to fairlife?

fairlife Core Power has 26g protein in a 14 fl oz bottle and fairlife nutrition plan has 30g in an 11.5 fl oz bottle. Swell has 25g protein in a 14 fl oz bottle and adds collagen peptides and colostrum, which neither fairlife shake lists as ingredients, in dessert flavors like Cake Batter and Cookies & Cream.

How does Swell compare to a milkshake?

A medium fast-food chocolate milkshake has about 650 calories, 85g sugar and 15g protein. Swell is built to taste like a milkshake with 25g protein and far less sugar.

Is Swell a meal replacement?

No. Swell is a protein shake meant as a snack, a post-workout drink or a dessert swap, not a complete meal.

Does Swell contain dairy?

Yes. Swell is made with milk protein and colostrum, which both come from milk. It is not suitable for people with a milk allergy.

Can I make Swell ice cream in a Ninja Creami?

Yes. Pour a bottle into a Creami pint, freeze it for 24 hours, then spin it on the Lite Ice Cream setting for a thick, scoopable treat.

Smoothie FAQ

Smoothie questions, answered straight.

What is a Swell Protein Smoothie?

A ready-to-drink smoothie with creamy fruit taste, 25g protein and less sugar in a 14 fl oz bottle.

What smoothie flavors does Swell make?

How is a Swell smoothie different from a Swell shake?
